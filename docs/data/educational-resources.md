# Educational resources

Recorded talks, webinars, and presentation material from the CARSS
consortium. See [Publications](publications.md) for peer-reviewed sources and
[News](../news.md) for press coverage.

## CARSS annual webinars

Recordings of CARSS's annual public webinars, from the
[CARSS USC YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CARSSUSC). Each covers
project status and progress at the time it was recorded — check a claim
against a current page on this site (e.g.
[Components](../components/index.md)) before treating it as up to date.
